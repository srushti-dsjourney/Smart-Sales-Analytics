# 📊 Smart Sales Analytics

A web-based **Sales Analytics and Forecasting Dashboard** built using Python, Flask, Pandas, Scikit-learn, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The system analyzes sales data and presents important business insights through an interactive dashboard, including revenue, orders, customers, products, cities, customer segments, and future sales predictions.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

**Smart Sales Analytics** is designed to help businesses understand their sales performance using data analytics and machine learning.

The dashboard converts raw sales data into meaningful insights such as:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Top-performing Products
- Sales by City
- Sales by Category
- Monthly Sales Trends
- Customer Spending
- Repeat vs One-time Customers
- Customer Segmentation
- Best and Lowest Performing Products
- Highest Revenue City
- Future Sales Forecast

The project also includes an interactive dashboard with filters for analyzing specific parts of the dataset.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Analyze historical sales data.
2. Identify important sales trends and patterns.
3. Understand customer purchasing behavior.
4. Identify high-performing and low-performing products.
5. Compare sales performance across cities.
6. Segment customers based on their spending.
7. Predict future sales using Machine Learning.
8. Present business insights through an interactive dashboard.

### 👥 Customer Analytics

Customer behavior is analyzed using:

---

### 📦 Product Insights

The system identifies:

- Best-selling Product
- Lowest-selling Product
- Sales generated by products
- Top-performing products
- Product performance comparison

---

### 🔮 Sales Forecasting

The project uses **Linear Regression** to predict future monthly sales.

The forecasting module provides:

- Predicted Sales
- Forecast Month
- Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
- R² Score

This helps businesses estimate future sales performance based on historical trends.

---

### 🔍 Interactive Filters

Users can filter dashboard results using:

- City
- Product
- Payment Mode
- Start Date
- End Date

A **Reset Filters** option is also available.

All major dashboard metrics and charts update according to the selected filters.
